Avidity 16 Apex — Carpool App

We have a carpool app set up for practices (https://aviditycarpool.dautovic.com) so everyone can see who is driving, claim seats, and check pickup details in one place.

Coaches: Ashley Krugh, Hannah Mullarkey
Practices: Tue & Thu 7:15–9:15 PM (St. John's Prep) · Sun 5:00–7:00 PM (DIS)
Open Avidity Carpool

1. First-Time Sign Up

  1. Open aviditycarpool.dautovic.com on your phone or computer.
  2. Tap First-time sign up (bottom-left of the login box).
  3. Enter the email address you provided for the team roster and choose a password (at least 6 characters).
  4. Tap Create Account.

Save to your phone home screen:
iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

2. Reading a Practice Card

  • Directions: Tap St. John's Prep ↗ (72 Spring St, Danvers) or DIS ↗ (150R Andover St, Danvers) to open Apple Maps or Google Maps.
  • All Set: Green All Set means both directions have enough seats and every going player has a ride.
  • To / From Tabs: On phones, switch between → To Practice and ← From Practice. On a computer, both appear side by side.

3. Attendance, Riding & Driving

  • Missing a practice: Tap [Name]: Going in the top-right of the card to switch to Out.
  • Claiming a ride: Tap Join on a car with open seats, or Both Ways to ride with the same driver both directions. Tap × next to your player's name to leave a car.
  • Offering to drive: Tap + I can drive, pick Both Ways / To / From, and select available player seats (1–7; 1 = driving solo for your own player).
  • If driving plans change: Tap Need sub so another parent can tap Take Over, or tap Drop to cancel your drive.

4. Notes, Texting, Events & Team Contacts

  • Notes & Texting: Tap + Note on any practice card to share pickup details, or use Text My Car / Text Carpool to message parents directly.
  • My Drives & Events: Use My Drives to export your driving schedule to your calendar, and Events to view tournaments.
  • Team Tab: Tap the Team tab to view parent phone numbers (call or text) and addresses (tap for Maps).
  • Update your info: Inside Team, tap Edit My Info to add or update your phone number, home address, or default seats.

If your email isn't recognized on sign-up or needs to be changed, reply with the email address you'd like to use.
