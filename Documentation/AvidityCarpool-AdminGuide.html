Avidity Carpool — Admin Guide

Manage team roster, carpool eligibility overrides, practice schedules, ride assignments, tournaments, and seasons.

Open aviditycarpool.dautovic.com ↗

1 Roster & Parent Accounts (Team Tab)

  • Add a Family: Tap Add Family / Parent to add a player and parent contact. Parents must sign up using the exact email address entered here.
  • Bulk Import: Tap Bulk Import to add multiple players and families at once.
  • Edit or Reset: Edit any player's name/jersey number, update a parent's contact info or Admin role, send a password reset email, or remove a parent.

2 Carpool Eligibility Override

By default, any player whose parent address includes Melrose is automatically included in the carpool (✓ Carpool).

  • Manual Override: Tap ✓ Carpool or + Carpool on any player in the Team tab to include a non-Melrose teammate or exclude a Melrose opt-out.
  • Where It Applies: Only ✓ Carpool players appear in practice ride headcounts, Still need a ride, and Text Carpool.

3 Managing Practices (Practices Tab)

  • Generate Schedule: Open Team → Season & Schedule → Generate Practices, choose a date range, and preview before creating.
  • Edit / Carpool Mode / Cancel / Remove: Tap the pencil icon on any practice card to change date, time, gym, status (e.g., Cancelled), or Carpool Needed (Both Ways, From Practice Only, To Practice Only, or No Carpool Needed), or tap the trash icon to delete it.

4 Ride Assignments & Overrides

  • Assign a Driver: Tap + I can drive or + Assign driver on any practice card and choose any drivable parent from the Driver (Admin) dropdown.
  • Override Need Sub / Adjust Seats / Remove Driver: On any car row, use the inline N seats ▾ dropdown next to the open-seat count to adjust available seats for that practice leg, tap Need Sub / Cancel Sub to override sub status, Take Over when a sub is needed, or Drop (with confirmation) to remove that driver from an individual practice leg without changing their roster Can drive status.
  • Assign or Remove Riders & Notes: Under Still need a ride, tap [Name] + to assign a player into any confirmed car with open seats, tap × next to any rider to remove them, or delete any practice note.

5 Tournaments & Seasons

  • Events Tab: Tap Load 26/27 Tournaments (when empty) or + Add Tournament to add, edit, or remove events.
  • New Season: Open Team → Season & Schedule → New Season, enter team/season details, select players to carry over, and create.
