Avidity Carpool — Admin Guide

Manage team roster, carpool eligibility overrides, practice schedules, ride assignments, tournaments, and seasons.

Open aviditycarpool.dautovic.com ↗

1 Roster, Guest Pool & Parent View (Team Tab)

  • Add a Family or Parent: Tap + Family / Parent (or + Parent on a player card) to add a player and parent contact. Parents sign up using the exact email address entered here.
  • Bulk Import & Edits: Tap Bulk Import to load multiple families, tap the pencil icon to edit a player's name/jersey number, or edit/reset password/remove any parent.
  • Guest Pool: Switch to Guest Pool inside Team and tap + Guest to register siblings or recurring guest riders (with parent contact/address for Stops) and optional recurring weekdays.
  • Parent View Preview: Tap Parent View (top bar on desktop, or inside the ••• menu on mobile) anytime to preview the exact non-admin parent experience, then tap Back to Admin to return.

2 Carpool Eligibility, Driver Engagement & Global Alerts

By default, any player whose parent address includes Melrose is automatically included in the carpool (✓ Carpool).

  • Manual Override: Tap ✓ Carpool or + Carpool on any player in Team → Roster to include a non-Melrose teammate or exclude a Melrose opt-out.
  • Global Push Alerts: Tap Alerts in Team → Roster to see how many carpool drivers have alerts active, globally toggle Instant "Needs Sub" Alerts and the 6:00 PM Night-Before Sweep, or tap Test Alert (Me) to verify push delivery and the top-bar Bell inbox without notifying other parents.
  • Driver Engagement: Switch to Drivers / Driver Engagement at the top of Team to review legs driven (All Season / Past / Upcoming), active drivers, and open upcoming legs—tap any driver's name for their ride history and riders.

3 Managing Practices (Practices Tab)

  • Add Single or Generate Schedule: Tap + Practice on the Practices tab to add a single practice event, or open Team → Season & Schedule → Generate Practices to batch-create a date range.
  • Edit / Carpool Mode / Cancel / Remove: Tap the pencil icon in any practice card header to change date, time, gym, status (e.g., Cancelled), or Carpool Needed (Both Ways, From Practice Only, To Practice Only, or No Carpool Needed), or tap the trash icon to delete it.

4 Ride Assignments, Guests & Overrides

  • Assign a Driver: Tap + I can drive or + Assign driver on any practice card and choose any drivable parent from the Driver (Admin) dropdown.
  • Override Need Sub / Adjust Seats / Remove Driver: On any car row, use N seats ▾ to adjust available seats for that leg, tap Need Sub / Cancel Sub (withdrawing or covering a sub automatically clears stale alerts from everyone's Bell inbox), Take Over, or Drop (with confirmation).
  • Assign or Remove Riders & Guests: Under Needs ride / Need ride, tap [Name] + to assign a player into a confirmed car (with a Both Ways option when that driver has open seats on both legs and the player isn't yet riding the other way), tap × to remove a rider, tap + Guest to add a pool or one-off guest, or delete any practice note.

5 Tournaments & Seasons

  • Events Tab: Tap Load 26/27 Tournaments (when empty) or + Tournament to add, edit, or remove events.
  • New Season: Open Team → Season & Schedule → New Season, enter team/season details, select players to carry over, and create.
