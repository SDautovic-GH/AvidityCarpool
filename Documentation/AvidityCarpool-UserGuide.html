Avidity Carpool

A quick guide for seeing who is driving, claiming a ride, or volunteering to drive—without digging through group texts.

Open aviditycarpool.dautovic.com ↗

1 First-Time Sign Up (30 Seconds)

  1. Open aviditycarpool.dautovic.com on your phone or computer.
  2. Tap First-time sign up at the bottom-left of the login box.
  3. Enter the email address you shared with the team admin and pick a password (6+ characters).
  4. Tap Create Account.
Save it like an app on your phone:
• iPhone (Safari): Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
• Android (Chrome): Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

2 Reading a Practice Card

  • Directions: Tap St. John's Prep ↗ or DIS ↗ to open Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps.
  • Status: All Set means both To Practice and From Practice have enough seats and every going player has a ride.
  • To / From Tabs (Phone): Tap → To Practice or ← From Practice to switch directions. On desktop, both show side by side.

3 Marking Your Player Going or Out

If your player will miss a practice, tap [Name]: Going in the top-right of that card to flip it to Out. This updates the headcount immediately and frees up their seat.

4 Claiming a Ride

  1. Find a car with open seats (for example, 2 open).
  2. Tap Join for that direction—or Both Ways (when shown) to ride with that driver both to and from practice.
  3. Need to switch? Tap × next to your player's name to leave the car.

5 Offering to Drive

  1. Tap + I can drive on a practice card.
  2. Choose Both Ways, To Practice, or From Practice.
  3. Pick how many total player seats you have (1–7; choosing 1 = Driving solo for your own player only).
  4. Tap Confirm Drive—your player is automatically added to your car.
Plans changed? Tap Need sub on your car so another parent can tap Take Over, or tap Drop to cancel your drive.

6 Notes, Contacts & Your Info

  • Practice Notes: Tap + Note on any practice card to share quick details (like pickup door or early arrival).
  • Directory Tab: View parent phone numbers (tap to call) and home addresses (tap for Maps).
  • Update Your Info: Inside Directory, tap Edit My Info to update your phone number, home address, or default seats.
  • My Drives Tab: Filters the list to only the practices you're driving.
