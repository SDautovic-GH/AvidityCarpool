Avidity Carpool

A quick guide for seeing who is driving, claiming a ride, or volunteering to drive—without digging through group texts.

Open aviditycarpool.dautovic.com ↗

1 First-Time Sign Up (30 Seconds)

  1. Open aviditycarpool.dautovic.com on your phone or computer.
  2. Tap First-time sign up at the bottom-left of the login box.
  3. Enter the email address you shared with the team admin and pick a password (6+ characters).
  4. Tap Create Account.
Save it like an app on your phone (recommended for push alerts):
• iPhone (Safari): Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
• Android (Chrome): Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

2 Reading a Practice Card

  • Directions: Tap St. John's Prep ↗ or DIS ↗ to open Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps.
  • Status: All Set means both To Practice and From Practice have enough seats and every going player has a ride (also shown on No Carpool events). One-way events show Carpool To Only or Carpool From Only, and any sub request shows an amber Needs Sub badge in the header.
  • To / From & Round-Trip View: Practices show → TO PRACTICE and ← FROM PRACTICE separately whenever rides are open or differ, and automatically consolidate into ⇄ TO & FROM once both ways are All Set with identical cars (tap To / From ▾ anytime to expand individual legs). Drivers covering both ways show a ⇄ Both Ways badge.
  • Cards vs. Scan: Switch between Cards (full details) and Scan (condensed monthly list with weekday filters, View ▾ to expand, and green ✓ All Set pills) on both Practices and My Drives—your preference is saved automatically.
  • Filters & Back to Top: Use All Upcoming to filter by month or past practices and toggle Needs Driver for uncovered practices. Tap All Upcoming, the active tab, or the floating ↑ button anytime to jump back to the top.

3 Marking Your Player Going or Out

If your player will miss a practice, tap [Name]: Going on that card to flip it to Out. This updates headcounts immediately and frees up their seat. Next to that pill, you'll also see your player's ride status at a glance (e.g., Riding with Sylvia or an amber Needs ride From warning if only one leg is covered).

4 Claiming a Ride

  1. Find a car with open seats (for example, 2 open).
  2. Tap Join for that direction—or Both Ways (when shown) to ride with that driver both to and from practice in one tap.
  3. Need to switch? Tap × next to your player's name to leave the car.
Guest Riders (G): Riders marked with a G badge are approved guest or sibling riders for that practice and count toward seat capacity and Stops. To request a guest rider, please reach out to Lee-Anne.

5 Offering to Drive

  1. Tap + I can drive on a practice card (or + Drive in Quick Scan).
  2. Choose Both Ways, To Only, or From Only.
  3. Pick how many total player seats you have (1–7; choosing 1 = Driving solo for your own player only).
  4. Optional: check Repeat for all upcoming [weekday] practices to volunteer for that weekday across the season.
  5. Tap Confirm Drive—your player is automatically added to your car.
Rider Stops (for drivers): Once teammates join your car, tap Stops on your car to see rider home addresses (1-tap Maps) and parent Call/Text links.
Plans changed? Use N seats ▾ next to your open-seat count to adjust seats for that practice, tap Need Sub so another parent can tap Take Over (tapping Cancel Sub clears the alert), or tap Drop to cancel your drive.

6 Push Alerts & Recent Alerts Inbox

  • Enable Push Alerts: Tap Enable Alerts (on iPhone, first add the app to your Home Screen via Share → Add to Home Screen) to receive urgent Needs Sub alerts and 6:00 PM night-before driver reminders (silent on off-days).
  • Bell Alerts Inbox: Tap the Bell icon in the top bar anytime to review recent alerts you may have missed or dismissed.
  • Opt Out Anytime: Toggle Push Alerts off or on anytime under Team → Edit My Info.

7 Notes, Texting, Events & Team Contacts

  • Practice Notes & Text My Car: Tap + Note to post an attributed note, or Text My Car to message just the parents in your car for that practice.
  • My Drives Tab: Shows only the practices you're driving (in Cards or Scan view), with an Add to Calendar (.ics) button.
  • Events Tab: Full tournament schedule with Maps links and 1-tap + Calendar downloads.
  • Team Tab & In-App Guide: View parent phone numbers (tap to call or Text), Text Team / Text Carpool, home addresses (tap for Maps), and Edit My Info. Tap ? in the header anytime for the in-app guide, or ••• on mobile to adjust zoom (80%–130%).
