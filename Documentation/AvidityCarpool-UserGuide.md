# Avidity Carpool — Parent User Guide

Welcome to **Avidity Carpool**! This app makes it quick and easy to see who is driving to and from volleyball practice, claim a seat for your player, or volunteer to drive—all in one place.

**App Link:** https://aviditycarpool.dautovic.com

---

## 1. First-Time Sign Up (Takes 30 Seconds)

1. Open **https://aviditycarpool.dautovic.com** on your phone or computer.
2. Tap **First-time sign up** at the bottom-left of the login box.
3. Enter the **email address** you gave the team admin and create a **password** (at least 6 characters).
4. Tap **Create Account**.

> **Forgot your password later?** Tap **Forgot password?** on the login screen and enter your email—or ask a team admin to send you a reset link.

### Recommended: Add to Your Phone's Home Screen (Like an App)
- **iPhone (Safari):** Tap the **Share** button (square with arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**. *(Required on iPhone to enable lock-screen push alerts.)*
- **Android (Chrome):** Tap the **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen** → **Add**.

---

## 2. Reading a Practice Card

Each card has a distinct header showing one practice date, time, headcount, and status:
- **Gym Link:** Tap **St. John's Prep ↗** or **DIS ↗** to open directions directly in Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps.
- **Green `All Set` Checkmark:** Both **To Practice** and **From Practice** have enough seats and every going player has a ride (also shown on **No Carpool** events).
- **Amber `Needs Sub` Badge:** Immediately flags when a driver has requested a sub on **To** or **From**.
- **To / From & Round-Trip Consolidation:**
  - Practices show **`→ TO PRACTICE`** and **`← FROM PRACTICE`** as separate sections whenever rides are still open, a leg needs a driver/sub, or the cars/riders differ between directions.
  - Once both ways are **All Set** with identical drivers and riders, the card automatically consolidates into a single **`⇄ TO & FROM`** section (tap **`To / From ▾`** in the header anytime to expand individual legs).
  - Any driver confirmed for both directions shows a **`⇄ Both Ways`** pill next to their name.
- **One-Way Carpool:** Special events where parents handle one direction themselves show **Carpool To Only** or **Carpool From Only**.

---

## 3. Marking Your Player "Going" or "Out"

If your player is missing practice, tap the **`[Player Name]: Going`** button on that practice card to switch it to **`Out`**.
- This immediately frees up their seat and removes them from the *"Needs ride"* list so drivers know the exact headcount.
- Next to `[Name]: Going`, you'll also see your player's ride status at a glance (e.g., *Riding with Sylvia*, *To: Lee-Anne · From: Tanya*, or an amber *Needs ride From* warning if only one leg is covered).

---

## 4. Claiming a Ride for Your Player

1. Find a car with open seats (for example, `2 open`).
2. Tap **Join** to claim a seat for that direction—or tap **Both Ways** (when shown) to put your player in the same driver's car both to and from practice in one tap.
3. Need to change plans? Tap the **`×`** next to your player's name to leave that car.
4. **Guest Riders (`G`):** Riders marked with a **`G`** badge are approved guest or sibling riders for that practice and count toward seat capacity and **Stops**. To request a guest rider, please reach out to **Lee-Anne**.

---

## 5. Offering to Drive

1. Tap **+ I can drive** on a practice card (or **+ Drive** in Quick Scan).
2. Choose **Both Ways**, **To Only**, or **From Only**.
3. Pick how many **total player seats** you have available (1–7; choosing **1** means *Driving solo* for your own player only).
4. Optional: check **Repeat for all upcoming [weekday] practices** to sign up for the whole season on that day of the week in one tap.
5. Tap **Confirm Drive**—your player is automatically placed in your car.

### Driving Tips & Plan Changes
- **Rider Stops (for drivers):** Once at least one teammate joins your car, tap **Stops** on your car row to see each rider's home address (1-tap Maps), parent **Call** / **Text** buttons, and a gym Maps link.
- **Adjust Seats for One Practice:** Use the inline **`N seats ▾`** dropdown next to the open-seat count on your car row to change seats for just that practice leg.
- **Need a Sub:** Tap **Need Sub** on your car to alert other carpool drivers so someone can tap **Take Over** (tapping **Cancel Sub** withdraws the request and clears the alert).
- **Cancel Driving:** Tap **Drop** to remove your car from that leg.

---

## 6. Push Alerts & Recent Alerts Inbox

- **Enable Push Alerts:** Tap **Enable Alerts** on the in-app prompt (or under **Team → Edit My Info**) to receive only two high-signal alerts:
  1. **Instant "Needs Sub" Alerts** when a confirmed driver needs coverage.
  2. **6:00 PM Night-Before Reminders** (silent on off-days) listing your riders for tomorrow if you're driving, or alerting carpool drivers if a leg still needs a driver.
- **iPhone Tip:** Add the app to your **Home Screen** first (*Share → Add to Home Screen*), then open it from your Home Screen and tap **Enable Alerts** → **Allow**.
- **Bell Alerts Inbox:** Tap the **Bell** icon in the top bar anytime to review recent alerts you may have missed or dismissed (works even without Home Screen push enabled).
- **Opt Out Anytime:** Toggle **Push Alerts** off or on anytime under **Team → Edit My Info**.

---

## 7. Notes, Texting & Navigation Tabs

- **+ Note & Text My Car:** Add an attributed note to any practice card (you can delete your own notes anytime), or tap **Text My Car** to start a group text with only the parents in your car for that practice.
- **Practices:** Filter by **All Upcoming** / month / **All Past**, toggle **Needs Driver**, and switch between **Cards** and **Scan** (condensed monthly list with weekday filters, **View ▾**, and green **✓ All Set** pills; your preference is saved automatically). Tap **All Upcoming**, the active tab, or the floating **`↑`** button anytime to jump back to the top.
- **My Drives:** Shows only practices where you are driving (in **Cards** or **Scan** view), with an **Add to Calendar** (`.ics`) button.
- **Events:** Full tournament schedule with Maps links and 1-tap **+ Calendar** (`.ics`) downloads.
- **Team:** Full team roster and contacts—tap any phone number to call or **Text**, use **Text Carpool** / **Text Team** at the top, tap any address for Maps, or tap **Edit My Info** to update your phone, address, default seats, or push alert preference.
- **In-App Guide & Display Zoom:** Tap **`?`** (mobile) or **Guide** (desktop) in the header to view this guide anytime; on mobile, tap **`•••`** to adjust text size (`80%–130%`) or sign out.
