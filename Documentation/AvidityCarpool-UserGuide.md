# Avidity Carpool — Parent User Guide

Welcome to **Avidity Carpool**! This app makes it quick and easy to see who is driving to and from volleyball practice, claim a seat for your player, or volunteer to drive—all in one place.

**App Link:** https://aviditycarpool.dautovic.com

---

## 1. First-Time Sign Up (Takes 30 Seconds)

1. Open **https://aviditycarpool.dautovic.com** on your phone or computer.
2. Tap **First-time sign up** at the bottom-left of the login box.
3. Enter the **email address** you gave the team admin and create a **password** (at least 6 characters).
4. Tap **Create Account**.

> **Forgot your password later?** Tap **Forgot password?** on the login screen and enter your email—or ask a team admin to send you a reset link.

### Optional: Add to Your Phone's Home Screen (Like an App)
- **iPhone (Safari):** Tap the **Share** button (square with arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
- **Android (Chrome):** Tap the **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen** → **Add**.

---

## 2. Reading a Practice Card

Each card shows one practice date, time, and gym:
- **Gym Link:** Tap **St. John's Prep ↗** or **DIS ↗** to open directions directly in Apple Maps (iPhone) or Google Maps.
- **Green `All Set` Checkmark:** Both **To Practice** and **From Practice** have enough seats and every going player has a ride.
- **Status Dot:**
  - **Green:** Covered — everyone going has a seat.
  - **Amber / Red:** Still need a driver, more seats, or a sub.
- **To / From Tabs (Phone):** On mobile, tap **→ To Practice** or **← From Practice** to switch directions. On a computer, both appear side by side.

---

## 3. Marking Your Player "Going" or "Out"

If your player is missing practice, tap the **`[Player Name]: Going`** button in the top-right of that practice card to switch it to **`Out`**.
- This immediately frees up their seat and removes them from the *"Still need a ride"* list so drivers know the exact headcount.

---

## 4. Claiming a Ride for Your Player

1. Find a car with open seats (for example, `2 open`).
2. Tap **Join** to claim a seat for that direction—or tap **Both Ways** (when shown) to put your player in the same driver's car both to and from practice in one tap.
3. Need to change plans? Tap the **`×`** next to your player's name to leave that car.

---

## 5. Offering to Drive

1. Tap **+ I can drive** on a practice card.
2. Choose **Both Ways**, **To Only**, or **From Only**.
3. Pick how many **total player seats** you have available (1–7; choosing **1** means *Driving solo* for your own player only).
4. Optional: check **Repeat for all upcoming [weekday] practices** to sign up for the whole season on that day of the week in one tap.
5. Tap **Confirm Drive**—your player is automatically placed in your car.

### If Your Plans Change After Signing Up to Drive
- **Need a Sub:** Tap **Need Sub** on your car if you want another parent to take over your spot. Any eligible parent can tap **Take Over** to step in.
- **Cancel Driving:** Tap **Drop** to remove your car from that leg.

---

## 6. Notes, Texting & Sharing

Need to share a quick update (like *"Meet by the north doors"* or *"Running 5 min late"*)?
- **+ Note:** Add an attributed note to any practice card (you can delete your own notes anytime).
- **Text My Car:** Opens a group text with only the driver and rider parents in your car for that practice.
- **Text Carpool:** Opens a group text with all Melrose carpool parents.
- **Share:** Copies or shares a clean text summary of the practice carpool.

---

## 7. Navigation Tabs: My Drives, Events & Team

- **Practices:** Upcoming practices with **All Upcoming** / **Needs Driver** filters and a **Past** toggle.
- **My Drives:** Shows only practices where you are driving, with an **Add My Drives to Calendar** button (`.ics` download).
- **Events:** Full tournament schedule with Maps links and 1-tap **+ Calendar** (`.ics`) downloads.
- **Team:** Full team roster and contacts:
  - **Call or Text:** Tap any phone number to call, tap **Text** to message one parent, or use **Text Carpool** / **Text Team** at the top.
  - **Directions:** Tap any home address to open Maps.
  - **Update your info:** Tap **Edit My Info** (or **Update** next to your name) to change your phone number, home address, or default car seats.
- **Display Zoom:** On mobile, tap **`⋯`** in the top-right header to adjust text size (`80%–130%`).
